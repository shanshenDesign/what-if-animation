# Story Plan: The Scrolling Trap

## Timing & Narrative Beats (~25 seconds / ~90 frames @ 30fps = 750 frames)
1. **Hook (0 - 120 f / ~4s)**: "What if scrolling for just two minutes before getting up pulls you into another cartoon world where thirty real-world minutes vanish?"
   - Shot: MCU on character staring at a glowing phone in a dark room.
2. **Setup (121 - 270 f / ~5s)**: "You sit on the edge of the bed, phone glowing in the dark. Just two quick clips, you tell yourself, before your feet hit the floor."
   - Shot: Wide bed setup, phone light illuminating the room.
3. **Escalation & Pull (271 - 450 f / ~6s)**: "Suddenly, the screen pulls you right through the glass like a whirlpool of digital neon. You land face-first in a bustling, absurd cartoon city built entirely of endless scrolling feeds and floating icons."
   - Shot: Camera push-in, phone flash / warp transition, wide reveal of the neon scrolling feed city.
4. **Exploration (451 - 600 f / ~5s)**: "You wander around, dodging giant thumbs and algorithm monsters for what feels like hours."
   - Shot: Tracking shot dodging giant thumbs and icons.
5. **Twist / Payoff (601 - 750 f / ~5s)**: "When you finally pry yourself away and scramble back out, your phone lights up. You check the clock. Thirty real-world minutes have vanished into thin air. And your feet are still right where you started."
   - Shot: Quick escape flash, MCU back on the bed looking at the phone clock, feet planted exactly where they started.
