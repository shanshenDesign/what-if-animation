In the year 3000, archaeologists uncovered a functional smartphone with one percent battery remaining.
Deep inside the radioactive ruins of a forgotten metropolis, a hazmat-suited team brushed away centuries of dust.
They pressed the power button, holding their breath as the cracked OLED screen flickered back to life.
The entire wasteland watched in awe as the Wi-Fi icon connected to a satellite that had orbited alone for a thousand years.
Robot sentinels lowered their laser blasters, mesmerized by the glowing notification bell.
But as the lead scientist reached out to tap the screen, a notification popped up.
Unlocking it triggered an ancient automated firmware update that instantly rebooted the planetary core.
And the robot uprising was canceled due to a required system restart.
