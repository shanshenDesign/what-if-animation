# Story Plan: The Last Phone Battery on Earth

## Timing & Narrative Beats (~25 seconds / ~750 frames @ 30fps)
1. **Hook (0 - 100 f)**: "In the year 3000, archaeologists uncovered a functional smartphone with one percent battery remaining."
   - Shot: MCU on glowing cracked phone screen in post-apocalyptic dust.
2. **Setup (101 - 250 f)**: "Deep inside the radioactive ruins of a forgotten metropolis, a hazmat-suited team brushed away centuries of dust."
   - Shot: Wide view of ruined skyscrapers and tiny bean-head archaeologists.
3. **Escalation (251 - 450 f)**: "They pressed the power button, holding their breath as the cracked OLED screen flickered back to life. Robot sentinels lowered their laser blasters, mesmerized."
   - Shot: Close-up on the power button click, screen glow illuminating towering robot eyes.
4. **Pivot (451 - 600 f)**: "But as the lead scientist reached out to tap the screen, a notification popped up."
   - Shot: Tracking camera pushing in over the scientist's shoulder toward the popup.
5. **Twist / Payoff (601 - 750 f)**: "Unlocking it triggered an ancient automated firmware update that instantly rebooted the planetary core. And the robot uprising was canceled due to a required system restart."
   - Shot: Massive white flash time jump, giant update progress bar across the sky, robots freezing with spinning loading wheels.
