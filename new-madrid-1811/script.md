## Full Voice-Over Text (143 words)

> December 16, 1811: the Mississippi River, the widest highway in America, suddenly ran backwards.
> Three great shocks rocked the New Madrid fault, and the strongest hit magnitude 7.9.
> Waves of ground rolled as far as Boston, and church bells rang on their own.
> But the worst was still coming, because the river was about to be flipped.
> Then on February 7, 1812, a magnitude 7.9 shock dropped whole farms under dark water.
> Settlers lost their fences, their crops and their chimneys, and New Madrid emptied in days.
> You see, the quake didn't split the land; it sank a valley into a lake.
> That sunken ground filled to become Reelfoot Lake, fifteen to twenty-five thousand acres wide.
> So Tennessee gained a shallow lake five to eighteen feet deep, and kept it forever.
> Two centuries later, Reelfoot Lake still remembers the night the whole river reversed.
